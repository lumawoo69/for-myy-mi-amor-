To My Favorite Person 💖

From the moment you entered my life, everything became softer, warmer, and more beautiful.


You became the person I think about before sleeping, the reason random songs suddenly feel emotional, and the reason my heart smiles for no reason sometimes.


You are honestly the prettiest feeling my heart has ever known.


Sometimes I wonder if you truly realize how special you are. Because the way you smile, the way you laugh, the way you simply exist — it changes everything around you.


You turned ordinary moments into memories. And somehow, even distance cannot stop my heart from finding you.


If love had a shape, I think it would look like galaxies, moonlight, roses, music, and your smile all mixed together.


You are my comfort. My favorite thought. My safest feeling.


And if I could choose again, I would still choose you every single time.


I want you to remember this forever: you deserve soft love, warm hugs, cute late night talks, and someone who genuinely cares about your heart.


And honestly? I care about your heart more than words can explain.


No matter where life takes us, a part of my heart will always belong to you.


Because loving you feels natural. Like stars belonging to the night sky.


You are my favorite universe.
